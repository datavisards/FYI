Global Air Quality Trends in 2023

Oct. 18, 2017 · John Green

Air quality (AQI) is a numerical scale used to measure how clean or polluted the air is. Lower values mean better air quality. AQI trends varied significantly across the globe in 2023.

Among all Asian cities, Tokyo had the best air quality, with an average AQI kept under 30. On the other hand, the situation in South America worsened: every single city in South America recorded a negative improvement rate.
